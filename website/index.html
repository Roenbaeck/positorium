01 / A DIFFERENT STARTING POINT

The world changes.
The evidence stays.

Two sources can disagree about the same thing. An assessment can change. Positorium preserves the claims and their context, so you can decide what is in effect—and see how you got there.

[01—03]

Disagreement is data.

Keep “high risk” and “low risk” together. An immutable posit records a claim without forcing a single version of the truth.

[02—03]

Context comes with it.

An assertion adds who said it, when they said it, and their certainty. Sources can revise their own assertions without erasing the trail.

[03—03]

Time has two sides.

Ask when a claim applies and when it was asserted. Reconstruct an earlier perspective with explicit temporal queries in Traqula.
